# 🏆 Nerd Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame recognizes individuals who have made significant contributions to the security, architecture, and educational quality of the **CMSForNerd** project.

## Current Honorees

* **Contribution:** Project Lead & Lead Architect.
* **Impact:** Orchestrated the v4.0.0 refactor, Zero-Global architecture, and laboratory standardization.

### Google Gemini & Antigravity
* **Contribution:** AI Modernization Engine & Autonomous Agent.
* **Impact:** Provided the logic refactoring, Glassmorphism integration, and technical documentation for PHP 8.4 standards.

## How to be Recognized
We follow the **Good Faith Disclosure** model. If you find a security flaw or provide a major logic optimization:
1. Report it via the `security-policy.php` protocol.
2. Provide a verified fix.
3. Pass the `composer lab-check` audit.
