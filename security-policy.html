🛡️ Security Policy & Disclosure

At CMSForNerd, we take the security of our users seriously. We follow a "Defense-in-Depth" strategy and appreciate the assistance of security researchers in keeping our laboratory safe.

1. Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not use public GitHub issues for security reports. Instead, email your findings to security@cmsfornerd.test.

  • RFC 2119 MUST: Include a detailed description of the vulnerability.
  • RFC 2119 MUST: Provide a Proof of Concept (PoC) or reproduction steps.
  • RFC 2119 SHOULD: Include potential impact and mitigation suggestions.

2. Our Commitment

For all valid reports submitted via the proper channels, we commit to:

72 Hours

Initial acknowledgment of receipt.

Full Transparency

Regular updates during the patch lifecycle.

Recognition

Credit in our Hall of Fame for "Good Faith" researchers.

3. Prohibited Actions

To remain in "Good Faith" status, researchers MUST NOT:

  • Attempt Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks.
  • Access, modify, or delete data not belonging to your test account.
  • Use Social Engineering or Phishing against laboratory students.
  • Exfiltrate data beyond what is necessary to prove the vulnerability.
